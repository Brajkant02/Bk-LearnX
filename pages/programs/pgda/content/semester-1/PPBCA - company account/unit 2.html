1. Introduction to Company Final Accounts

Company Final Accounts वे financial statements हैं जिनके माध्यम से किसी company की एक accounting period के अंत में financial performance और financial position को प्रस्तुत किया जाता है।

Company के final accounts का उद्देश्य केवल profit निकालना नहीं है, बल्कि shareholders, management, creditors, investors, government और अन्य stakeholders को company की financial information एक व्यवस्थित और comparable form में देना भी है।

Exam Definition: Company Final Accounts वे final financial statements हैं जो accounting year के अंत में company के profit/loss तथा assets, liabilities और equity की स्थिति को निर्धारित एवं प्रस्तुत करने के लिए तैयार किए जाते हैं।

Financial Statements में मुख्य statements

Statementमुख्य उद्देश्य
Balance Sheetएक निश्चित date पर company की financial position बताती है।
Statement of Profit and Lossएक accounting period में income, expenses और profit/loss बताता है।
Cash Flow StatementCash और cash equivalents के inflow/outflow की जानकारी देता है, जहाँ applicable हो।
Statement of Changes in EquityEquity में हुए changes को दिखाता है, जहाँ applicable हो।
Notes to AccountsFinancial statements के line items की detailed information और disclosures देता है।
ध्यान दें: Company financial statements की statutory presentation Companies Act, applicable Accounting Standards/Ind AS और Schedule III requirements से प्रभावित होती है। Exam में syllabus के अनुसार prescribed format को प्राथमिकता दें।

2. Preparation and Presentation of Company Final Accounts

Company final accounts बनाते समय केवल ledger balances को जोड़ना पर्याप्त नहीं होता। Accounts को appropriate classification, adjustments, accounting standards और prescribed presentation format के अनुसार तैयार किया जाता है।

Trial Balance प्राप्त करना
सभी ledger accounts के closing balances को Trial Balance से लिया जाता है।
Adjustments की पहचान
Closing inventory, depreciation, outstanding expenses, prepaid expenses, accrued income, provision आदि adjustments को identify किया जाता है।
Income और Expenses का निर्धारण
Revenue, other income तथा सभी applicable expenses को classify किया जाता है।
Statement of Profit and Loss
Income और expenses के आधार पर profit before tax तथा profit after tax determine किया जाता है।
Appropriation / Retained Profit
Exam-oriented traditional presentation में profit appropriation जैसे reserve transfer और dividend को अलग से समझाया जा सकता है।
Balance Sheet
Equity and liabilities तथा assets को prescribed classification में प्रस्तुत किया जाता है।
Notes and Comparative Information
Relevant notes और applicable previous-year comparative figures को presentation requirements के अनुसार शामिल किया जाता है।
Basic Flow:
Trial Balance → Adjustments → Statement of Profit & Loss → Profit/Appropriation → Balance Sheet → Notes to Accounts

Presentation के basic principles

  • Items को appropriate heads और sub-heads में classify किया जाता है।
  • Material information को clearly disclose किया जाता है।
  • Assets, liabilities और equity को proper order में show किया जाता है।
  • Figures के साथ applicable comparative information और notes दिए जाते हैं।
  • Financial statements को applicable Accounting Standards/Ind AS और Companies Act requirements के अनुरूप तैयार किया जाता है।

3. Company Balance Sheet

Balance Sheet एक particular date पर company की financial position का statement है। इसमें मुख्य रूप से Equity, Liabilities और Assets दिखाई जाती हैं।

Accounting Equation:
Assets = Equity + Liabilities

Company Balance Sheet का broad structure

BALANCE SHEET OF ABC LIMITED
as at 31 March 20XX
ParticularsAmount (₹)
I. Equity and Liabilities
1. Shareholders' Funds / Equity
2. Non-current Liabilities
3. Current Liabilities
Total Equity and LiabilitiesXXX
II. Assets
1. Non-current Assets
2. Current Assets
Total AssetsXXX
Exam Point: Balance Sheet को “as at” किसी specific date पर बनाया जाता है क्योंकि यह उस date पर financial position को represent करती है।

4. Forms and Contents of Company Balance Sheet

4.1 Form / Presentation

Modern company financial statements generally use a vertical presentation. Indian company reporting में Schedule III prescribed structure महत्वपूर्ण है; exact division depends on the applicable accounting framework.

Equity & Liabilities

  • Share Capital / applicable equity classification
  • Reserves and surplus / Other Equity
  • Non-current borrowings
  • Deferred tax liabilities, where applicable
  • Long-term provisions
  • Trade payables
  • Other current liabilities
  • Short-term provisions

Assets

  • Property, Plant and Equipment
  • Intangible Assets
  • Capital Work-in-Progress
  • Non-current investments
  • Long-term loans/advances, where applicable
  • Inventories
  • Trade receivables
  • Cash and cash equivalents
  • Short-term loans/advances and other current assets

Important contents explained

ItemMeaning / treatment
Share CapitalCompany द्वारा issued/subscribed share capital की relevant details.
Reserves / Other EquityRetained earnings और applicable reserves/equity components.
BorrowingsLoans और other financing liabilities, current/non-current classification के अनुसार.
Trade PayablesGoods/services के suppliers को payable amounts.
PPELand, building, plant, machinery, furniture आदि tangible long-term assets, applicable depreciation के बाद.
InventoryStock of goods, raw materials, work-in-progress आदि, applicable valuation basis के अनुसार.
Trade ReceivablesCustomers से recover होने वाली amounts.
Cash & Cash EquivalentsCash, bank balances और applicable short-term highly liquid investments.
Classification Rule: किसी item को current या non-current classify करने के लिए applicable Schedule III और accounting framework के criteria देखे जाते हैं। केवल नाम देखकर classification नहीं करना चाहिए।

5. Profit and Loss A/c / Statement of Profit and Loss

Statement of Profit and Loss एक accounting period के दौरान company की income और expenses को दिखाता है और अंत में profit या loss determine करता है।

STATEMENT OF PROFIT AND LOSS OF ABC LIMITED
for the year ended 31 March 20XX
ParticularsAmount (₹)
Revenue from OperationsXXX
Other IncomeXXX
Total IncomeXXX
Expenses:
Cost of materials / Purchases / relevant operating costsXXX
Employee benefits expenseXXX
Finance costsXXX
Depreciation and amortisation expenseXXX
Other expensesXXX
Total ExpensesXXX
Profit Before TaxXXX
Tax ExpenseXXX
Profit After TaxXXX
Profit Before Tax = Total Income − Total Expenses
Profit After Tax = Profit Before Tax − Tax Expense

Important heads

  • Revenue from Operations: company की main operating activities से earned revenue.
  • Other Income: operating revenue के अलावा applicable income, जैसे interest/dividend आदि.
  • Employee Benefits Expense: salaries, wages और related employee costs.
  • Finance Costs: borrowing-related interest और applicable finance charges.
  • Depreciation & Amortisation: depreciable tangible assets और applicable intangible assets की periodic charge.
  • Other Expenses: administrative, selling/distribution और अन्य applicable expenses.

6. Profit & Loss Appropriation Account

Profit & Loss Appropriation Account का उपयोग profit की appropriation यानी profit के distribution/allocation को दिखाने के लिए traditional accounting presentation में किया जाता है।

Important modern-reporting note: Current statutory company financial statements में profit का presentation applicable Schedule III/Ind AS/AS framework के अनुसार होता है। इसलिए exam में यदि “P&L Appropriation A/c” specifically पूछा गया है, तो traditional format follow करें; इसे statutory Statement of Profit and Loss से अलग समझें।

7. Company Final Accounts बनाने की Practical Procedure

Step 1 — Trial Balance पढ़ें

Trial Balance के प्रत्येक item को पहचानें कि वह income, expense, asset, liability या equity से संबंधित है।

Step 2 — Adjustments apply करें

AdjustmentTypical effect
Outstanding ExpenseExpense बढ़ेगा + Current Liability बढ़ेगी.
Prepaid ExpenseExpense घटेगा + Current Asset बढ़ेगा.
DepreciationP&L में expense + Asset carrying amount में reduction.
Accrued IncomeIncome बढ़ेगा + Asset/receivable बढ़ेगा.
Income received in advanceCurrent period income घटेगा + liability बन सकती है.
Closing InventoryApplicable cost of sales calculation और Balance Sheet में current asset के रूप में consideration.

Step 3 — Statement of Profit & Loss तैयार करें

Revenue + other income से total income निकालें। फिर applicable expenses deduct करके Profit Before Tax और Profit After Tax determine करें।

Step 4 — Appropriation

यदि question में reserve transfer, dividend या retained profit related information दी गई है, तो traditional P&L Appropriation format में show करें।

Step 5 — Balance Sheet तैयार करें

Equity and liabilities तथा assets को prescribed classification के अनुसार arrange करें और total दोनों sides/equation को verify करें।

8. Numerical Practice – I (Simple Company Final Accounts)

Question: XYZ Ltd. की following information से Statement of Profit & Loss तथा Balance Sheet का simplified form तैयार कीजिए।

Particulars₹
Revenue from Operations8,00,000
Other Income20,000
Employee Benefits Expense1,50,000
Other Expenses1,30,000
Depreciation40,000
Finance Cost20,000
Tax Expense50,000
Share Capital5,00,000
Opening Retained Earnings80,000
Borrowings2,00,000
Trade Payables1,00,000
Cash & Bank2,00,000
Trade Receivables2,50,000
Inventory1,20,000
PPE3,10,000
Working:
Total Income = ₹8,00,000 + ₹20,000 = ₹8,20,000
Total Expenses = ₹1,50,000 + ₹1,30,000 + ₹40,000 + ₹20,000 = ₹3,40,000
Profit Before Tax = ₹8,20,000 − ₹3,40,000 = ₹4,80,000
Profit After Tax = ₹4,80,000 − ₹50,000 = ₹4,30,000
Closing Retained Earnings = ₹80,000 + ₹4,30,000 = ₹5,10,000

Other Income30,000
Salaries2,00,000

Adjustments

  • Salary outstanding = ₹20,000
  • Rent prepaid = ₹10,000
  • Additional depreciation = ₹10,000
Step 1: Adjusted Salaries
₹2,00,000 + ₹20,000 = ₹2,20,000

Step 2: Adjusted Rent
₹1,00,000 − ₹10,000 = ₹90,000

Step 3: Adjusted Depreciation

10. Numerical Practice Questions

Practice 1

ABC Ltd. के Revenue from Operations ₹12,00,000, Other Income ₹40,000, Employee Benefits ₹2,50,000, Finance Cost ₹50,000, Depreciation ₹60,000 और Other Expenses ₹2,00,000 हैं। Tax Expense ₹90,000 है। Statement of Profit and Loss तैयार करके Profit Before Tax और Profit After Tax निकालिए।

Practice 2

XYZ Ltd. के salaries ₹3,00,000 हैं। ₹25,000 salary outstanding है। Rent ₹1,20,000 है जिसमें ₹15,000 prepaid है। Depreciation ₹70,000 तथा additional depreciation ₹10,000 है। Revenue ₹15,00,000 और Other Income ₹50,000 है। Adjusted expenses determine कीजिए।

Practice 3

A Ltd. के पास Share Capital ₹8,00,000, General Reserve ₹1,50,000, Borrowings ₹3,00,000, Trade Payables ₹1,00,000, PPE ₹6,00,000, Inventory ₹2,00,000, Trade Receivables ₹2,50,000 और Cash ₹1,00,000 हैं। Balance Sheet के major heads बनाकर totals check कीजिए और missing balancing figure identify कीजिए।

Practice 4 — Appropriation

एक company का Profit After Tax ₹6,00,000 है और opening retained profit ₹1,00,000 है। General Reserve में ₹1,00,000 transfer और dividend ₹2,50,000 दिया गया है। Traditional Profit & Loss Appropriation Account बनाकर closing retained profit निकालिए।

Practice 4 Answer:
Amount available = ₹1,00,000 + ₹6,00,000 = ₹7,00,000
Less: General Reserve ₹1,00,000 + Dividend ₹2,50,000 = ₹3,50,000
Closing retained profit = ₹3,50,000
