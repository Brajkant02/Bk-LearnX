1. Meaning of Share

Share company की share capital का एक unit है। जब company अपनी capital को छोटे-छोटे units में divide करती है, तो प्रत्येक unit को share कहा जाता है। Shareholder को share रखने के बदले company में उसके ownership interest से संबंधित rights मिलते हैं, subject to the company's constitutional documents and applicable law.

Exam Definition: A share is a unit into which the share capital of a company is divided.

Example

यदि किसी company की share capital ₹10,00,000 है और प्रत्येक share का face value ₹10 है, तो total shares:

Number of Shares = Share Capital ÷ Face Value
₹10,00,000 ÷ ₹10 = 1,00,000 shares

Important Share Terms

TermMeaning
Face Value / Nominal ValueShare की stated nominal value, जैसे ₹10 per share.
Issue Priceजिस price पर company investor को share issue करती है।
Market PriceMarket में share का prevailing trading price, जहाँ applicable हो।
Share CapitalShares के issue से company द्वारा raised capital.
ShareholderCompany का member जिसने shares hold किए हैं, subject to applicable legal framework.

2. Types of Shares

Companies के share capital को broadly Equity Shares और Preference Shares में समझा जाता है।

Equity Shares

Equity shareholders company के residual owners होते हैं। Dividend generally fixed नहीं होता और voting rights applicable law के अनुसार हो सकते हैं।

Preference Shares

Preference shares को dividend के payment और capital repayment में equity shares पर preferential rights प्राप्त होते हैं, subject to terms and applicable law.

Preference Shares की common classifications

TypeBasic Meaning
CumulativeUnpaid preference dividend future years में accumulate हो सकता है, subject to terms.
Non-CumulativeUnpaid dividend सामान्यतः अगले year के claim के रूप में accumulate नहीं होता।
ParticipatingSpecified conditions में preference dividend के अतिरिक्त surplus profits/assets में participation का right हो सकता है।
Non-ParticipatingParticipation right नहीं होता, except the preferential rights specified.
ConvertibleSpecified terms के अनुसार equity shares में conversion का right हो सकता है।
Non-ConvertibleEquity shares में conversion का right नहीं होता।
RedeemableApplicable terms और law के अनुसार company द्वारा redeem किए जा सकते हैं।
Exam Note: “Types of shares” में Equity और Preference Shares लिखना मुख्य classification है। Preference shares की sub-types question के requirement के अनुसार लिखें।

3. Issue of Shares at Par

जब company share को उसके face value के बराबर price पर issue करती है, तो इसे Issue at Par कहते हैं।

Issue Price = Face Value
Example: Face Value ₹10, Issue Price ₹10 → Issue at Par

4. Issue of Shares at Premium

जब shares को face value से अधिक price पर issue किया जाता है, तो excess amount को Securities Premium के रूप में treat किया जाता है, subject to applicable law.

Issue Price = Face Value + Premium
Example: Face Value ₹10 + Premium ₹2 = Issue Price ₹12

Journal Entry

EntryDebit (₹)Credit (₹)
Share Application / Allotment / Call A/c Dr.
To Share Capital A/c
To Securities Premium A/c		Issue amountFace value
Premium
Example: 5,000 shares of ₹10 each are issued at ₹12 per share.
Share Capital = 5,000 × ₹10 = ₹50,000
Securities Premium = 5,000 × ₹2 = ₹10,000
Total amount received = ₹60,000

5. Issue of Shares at Discount

जब share को face value से कम price पर issue किया जाता है, तो उसे issue at discount कहा जाता है।

Discount = Face Value − Issue Price
Current-law caution: Companies Act, 2013 के under shares at discount की issue पर general restriction है और केवल specifically permitted situations में ही discount issue allowed हो सकता है. इसलिए exam में traditional accounting treatment और current legal restriction दोनों को context के अनुसार समझें।

Traditional Accounting Illustration

यदि किसी academic question में ₹10 share को ₹9 पर issue करने की अनुमति/assumption दी गई हो:

EntryDebitCredit
Bank A/c Dr.₹9
Discount on Issue of Share A/c Dr.₹1
To Share Capital A/c₹10

यह entry केवल traditional academic illustration है; actual issue must comply with applicable company law.

6. Calls in Arrear

जब shareholder को company द्वारा किसी call का payment करना होता है लेकिन वह due date तक amount pay नहीं करता, तो unpaid amount को Calls in Arrear कहा जाता है।

Calls in Arrear = Amount Called − Amount Actually Received

Accounting Treatment

EntryDebitCredit
Calls in Arrear A/c Dr.
To Relevant Call A/c		Unpaid amountUnpaid amount
Example: 1,000 shares पर ₹3 final call due था, लेकिन ₹2,400 ही received हुआ।
Calls in Arrear = ₹3,000 − ₹2,400 = ₹600.

Applicable terms के अनुसार arrears पर interest charge किया जा सकता है।

7. Calls in Advance

जब shareholder किसी call की due date से पहले company को future call का amount pay कर देता है, तो उसे Calls in Advance कहा जाता है।

Calls in Advance = Amount received before it becomes due

Accounting Treatment

EntryDebitCredit
Bank A/c Dr.
To Calls in Advance A/c		Amount receivedAmount received
When call becomes due:
Calls in Advance A/c Dr.
To Relevant Call A/c		Amount adjustedAmount adjusted
Calls in Advance company की share capital तब तक नहीं बनती जब तक relevant call due और properly adjusted न हो। Interest, voting rights और other treatment applicable law तथा terms के अनुसार होंगे।

8. Forfeiture of Shares

Forfeiture of shares का अर्थ है company द्वारा defaulting shareholder के shares को prescribed procedure और applicable articles/law के अनुसार cancel/forfeit करना, generally because a due call remains unpaid.

Conditions / Basic Process

Relevant call की payment due और unpaid होती है।
Company prescribed notice/procedure follow करती है।
Default continue रहने पर competent authority/board applicable articles के अनुसार forfeiture resolve करता है।
Forfeited shares की amount received और unpaid amount का accounting treatment किया जाता है।

Basic Journal Entry — Shares Issued at Par

ParticularsDebitCredit
Share Capital A/c Dr.
To Calls in Arrear / Relevant Call A/c
To Share Forfeiture A/c		Called-up capitalUnpaid amount
Amount already received
Example: ₹10 share पर ₹8 called-up है। Shareholder ने ₹5 pay किया और ₹3 call unpaid है। Forfeiture पर:
Share Capital Dr. ₹8
To Calls in Arrear ₹3
To Share Forfeiture ₹5
Note: Premium unpaid होने या shares issued at premium होने पर entry की treatment facts के अनुसार बदल सकती है। Numerical में premium receipt status ध्यान से देखें।

9. Reissue of Forfeited Shares

Forfeited shares को company prescribed conditions के अनुसार फिर से issue कर सकती है। Reissue price और forfeited amount के accounting treatment से capital profit determine हो सकता है।

Maximum Discount on Reissue = Amount Forfeited on those Shares, subject to applicable rules.

Basic Reissue Entries

SituationJournal Entry
Reissue at parBank A/c Dr. → To Share Capital A/c
Reissue at discountBank A/c Dr. + Share Forfeiture A/c Dr. → To Share Capital A/c
Transfer of surplus forfeitureShare Forfeiture A/c Dr. → To Capital Reserve A/c

Reissue Numerical Illustration

₹10 का share ₹6 paid-up था और ₹4 पहले receive हो चुका था। Share forfeited हुआ क्योंकि ₹2 call unpaid था। बाद में share ₹5 पर fully paid reissue किया गया।

Forfeiture amount = ₹4
Reissue discount = ₹10 − ₹5 = ₹5, लेकिन यहाँ share केवल ₹6 called-up था; question facts के अनुसार reissue को ₹5 paid-up/fully paid बनाना हो तो exact called-up and reissue terms carefully interpret करें।

Exam rule: Reissue discount forfeited amount से अधिक नहीं होना चाहिए, subject to the applicable accounting/legal framework.
Better numerical approach: पहले called-up amount, amount received, unpaid call, reissue price और face value को separately write करें; फिर forfeiture और reissue entries बनाएं।

10. Bonus Issue

Bonus Issue का अर्थ है company द्वारा existing eligible shareholders को, specified terms और applicable law के अनुसार, accumulated reserves/securities premium या other permitted sources को capitalise करके additional shares देना। इसे generally free shares भी कहा जाता है क्योंकि shareholder से issue price की fresh cash consideration नहीं ली जाती।

Bonus Shares = Existing Eligible Shares × Bonus Ratio

Example

यदि company 1:2 bonus issue करती है, तो प्रत्येक 2 existing shares पर 1 bonus share मिलेगा। यदि shareholder के पास 10,000 shares हैं:

Bonus Shares = 10,000 × 1/2 = 5,000 shares

Sources commonly used for Bonus Issue

  • Free reserves, where permitted.
  • Securities premium account, where permitted.
  • Capital redemption reserve account, where permitted.
Important: Bonus issue में shareholder को company से cash receipt नहीं होती। Reserves/equity के एक component को share capital में capitalise किया जाता है, subject to applicable law and conditions.

Basic Accounting Concept

StageTypical Entry Concept
Bonus declared / approvedRelevant Reserve / Permitted Account Dr. → To Bonus to Shareholders / Bonus Issue related account
Bonus shares issuedBonus related account Dr. → To Share Capital A/c

11. Solved Numerical Practice

Numerical 1 — Issue at Premium

ABC Ltd. ने 20,000 equity shares of ₹10 each at ₹12 per share issue किए। पूरी राशि एक साथ received हुई। Entries और amounts निकालिए।

Share Capital = 20,000 × ₹10 = ₹2,00,000
Securities Premium = 20,000 × ₹2 = ₹40,000
Bank received = ₹2,40,000

To Share Capital A/c
To Securities Premium A/c		2,40,0002,00,000
40,000

Numerical 2 — Calls in Arrear

5,000 shares पर final call ₹2 per share due था। Company को ₹9,200 प्राप्त हुए। Calls in Arrear निकालिए।

Amount Due = 5,000 × ₹2 = ₹10,000
Amount Received = ₹9,200
Calls in Arrear = ₹800

Numerical 3 — Calls in Advance

2,000 shares पर future call ₹3 per share due था। एक shareholder ने ₹6,000 पहले ही pay कर दिए। Calls in Advance = ?

Calls in Advance = 2,000 × ₹3 = ₹6,000

Numerical 4 — Bonus Issue

Company ने 1:4 bonus issue घोषित किया। एक shareholder के पास 20,000 shares हैं। उसे कितने bonus shares मिलेंगे?

Bonus Shares = 20,000 × 1/4 = 5,000 shares
Total shares after bonus = 20,000 + 5,000 = 25,000 shares

12. Practice Questions

Practice 1

30,000 shares of ₹10 each are issued at ₹13 per share. Calculate share capital and securities premium.

Practice 2

8,000 shares पर ₹2 final call due था। ₹14,500 received हुए। Calls in arrear निकालिए।

Practice 3

5,000 shares पर ₹4 future call shareholder ने due date से पहले pay किया। Calls in Advance निकालिए।

Practice 4

एक ₹10 share पर ₹7 called-up है। ₹5 received और ₹2 unpaid है। Forfeiture entry तैयार कीजिए।

Practice 5

Company 1:3 bonus issue करती है। Shareholder के पास 12,000 shares हैं। Bonus और post-bonus total shares निकालिए।

Answers:
1 → Share Capital ₹3,00,000; Securities Premium ₹90,000

3 → Calls in Advance ₹20,000
