1. Meaning of Debenture

Debenture company द्वारा borrowed capital raise करने का एक instrument है। Debenture holder company का creditor होता है, owner नहीं। Debenture पर generally specified rate से interest payable होता है और principal amount को agreed terms के अनुसार repay/redemption किया जाता है।

Exam Definition: A debenture is an instrument acknowledging a debt of a company, carrying terms relating to interest and repayment/redemption.

Important Terms

TermMeaning
Face ValueDebenture की nominal value, जैसे ₹100 per debenture.
Issue Priceजिस amount पर debenture investor को issue किया जाता है।
Interest Rate / CouponDebenture पर payable specified interest rate.
Debenture HolderCompany का creditor जिसने debentures hold किए हैं।
RedemptionDebentures की maturity/terms के अनुसार repayment.
Annual Interest = Face Value of Debentures × Rate of Interest

3. Issue of Debentures at Par

जब debenture को उसके face value के बराबर amount पर issue किया जाता है, तो इसे Issue at Par कहते हैं।

Issue Price = Face Value
₹100 debenture issued at ₹100 = Issue at Par

Journal Entry

ParticularsDebitCredit
Bank A/c Dr.
To Debentures A/c		Issue amountFace value
Example: 5,000 debentures of ₹100 each issued at par.
Bank received = 5,000 × ₹100 = ₹5,00,000.

4. Issue of Debentures at Premium

जब debentures को face value से अधिक price पर issue किया जाता है, तो excess amount को Premium on Issue of Debentures कहा जाता है।

Issue Price = Face Value + Premium

Journal Entry

ParticularsDebitCredit
Bank A/c Dr.
To Debentures A/c
To Securities Premium / Premium on Issue of Debentures A/c		Total amount receivedFace value
Premium
Example: 10,000 debentures of ₹100 each issued at ₹105.
Face value = ₹10,00,000
Premium = ₹50,000
Total received = ₹10,50,000

5. Issue of Debentures at Discount

जब debentures को face value से कम amount पर issue किया जाता है, तो difference को Discount on Issue of Debentures कहा जाता है।

Discount = Face Value − Issue Price

Journal Entry

ParticularsDebitCredit
Bank A/c Dr.
Discount on Issue of Debentures A/c Dr.
To Debentures A/c		Net cash
Discount		Face value
Example: 2,000 debentures of ₹100 issued at ₹95.
Cash received = ₹1,90,000
Discount = ₹10,000
Debenture liability = ₹2,00,000.

6. Calls in Arrear and Calls in Advance on Debentures

Calls in Arrear

जब debenture issue की instalment/call due हो जाती है लेकिन holder ने due date तक payment नहीं किया, तो unpaid amount को Calls in Arrear कहा जा सकता है।

Calls in Arrear = Amount Due − Amount Received
EntryDebitCredit
Calls in Arrear A/c Dr.
To Relevant Debenture Call A/c		Unpaid amountUnpaid amount

Calls in Advance

जब debenture holder future call/installment का amount due date से पहले pay करता है, तो उसे Calls in Advance कहा जाता है।

StageEntry
Advance receivedBank A/c Dr. → To Calls in Advance A/c
Call becomes dueCalls in Advance A/c Dr. → To Relevant Debenture Call A/c
Interest, adjustment और other rights का treatment issue terms तथा applicable law पर depend करता है।

7. Interest on Debentures

Debenture holder company का creditor होता है। Debenture terms के अनुसार company को specified rate पर interest pay करना होता है। यह company के लिए finance cost/expense होता है।

Interest = Face Value of Debentures × Rate × Time

Example

₹5,00,000 के 10% debentures पर पूरे वर्ष का interest:

Interest = ₹5,00,000 × 10% × 1 = ₹50,000

Journal Entries

Debenture Interest A/c Dr.

Debentureholders / Interest Payable A/c Dr.

8. Loss on Issue of Debentures

जब debentures issue करने में company को ऐसा loss incur होता है जो issue price/terms के कारण उत्पन्न होता है, उसे Loss on Issue of Debentures कहा जाता है। यह commonly तब दिखाई देता है जब debentures discount पर issue हों और/या redemption premium पर redeem होने हों।

Loss on Issue = Discount on Issue + Premium on Redemption
(जब दोनों applicable हों)

Example

₹10,00,000 debentures 4% discount पर issue किए गए और 5% premium पर redeem होने हैं।

Discount = ₹10,00,000 × 4% = ₹40,000
Redemption Premium = ₹10,00,000 × 5% = ₹50,000
Total Loss on Issue = ₹90,000

Basic Journal Entry at Issue

ParticularsDebitCredit
Bank A/c Dr.
Loss on Issue of Debentures A/c Dr.
To Debentures A/c
To Premium on Redemption of Debentures A/c		Cash + LossFace Value
Redemption Premium
Writing point: Loss on issue is generally a deferred expenditure/adjustment item in traditional exam problems and is written off over the relevant period according to the question and applicable accounting framework.

9. Redemption of Debentures

Redemption of debentures का अर्थ है company द्वारा debentureholders को debentures की amount repay करके liability समाप्त करना। Redemption issue terms के अनुसार par या premium पर हो सकती है।

Redemption at Par

Amount payable = Face Value
EntryDebitCredit
Debentures A/c Dr.
To Debentureholders A/c		Face valueAmount due
Debentureholders A/c Dr.
To Bank A/c		Amount payableCash paid

Redemption at Premium

Amount Payable = Face Value + Premium on Redemption
ParticularsDebitCredit
Debentures A/c Dr.
Premium on Redemption of Debentures A/c Dr.
To Debentureholders A/c		Face value
Premium		Total amount payable

Redemption Numerical

10,000 debentures of ₹100 each are redeemable at 105%.

Face Value = 10,000 × ₹100 = ₹10,00,000
Premium = 5% of ₹10,00,000 = ₹50,000
Amount payable = ₹10,50,000

Common Redemption Methods

Lump-sum Redemption
पूरी debenture liability एक specified date पर redeem की जाती है।
Instalment Redemption
Terms के अनुसार liability portions/installments में redeem की जाती है।
Conversion
Convertible debentures को specified terms पर another security में convert किया जा सकता है।
Purchase in Open Market
जहाँ legally permitted हो, company अपने debentures market से purchase करके cancel/hold कर सकती है।

10. Solved Numerical Practice

Numerical 1 — Issue at Premium

5,000 debentures of ₹100 each are issued at ₹108 per debenture. Calculate cash received and premium.

Face Value = 5,000 × ₹100 = ₹5,00,000
Premium = 5,000 × ₹8 = ₹40,000
Cash received = ₹5,40,000

Numerical 2 — Issue at Discount

4,000 debentures of ₹100 each are issued at 96%. Calculate discount and cash received.

Face Value = ₹4,00,000
Issue Price = 96% × ₹4,00,000 = ₹3,84,000
Discount = ₹16,000

Numerical 3 — Interest

₹8,00,000, 12% debentures are outstanding for one full year. Calculate annual interest.

Interest = ₹8,00,000 × 12% = ₹96,000

Numerical 4 — Loss on Issue

₹20,00,000 debentures are issued at 5% discount and redeemable at 8% premium. Calculate loss on issue.

Discount = 5% of ₹20,00,000 = ₹1,00,000
Redemption Premium = 8% of ₹20,00,000 = ₹1,60,000
Loss on Issue = ₹2,60,000

Numerical 5 — Redemption at Premium

12,000 debentures of ₹100 each are redeemable at 103%. Calculate amount payable.

Face Value = ₹12,00,000
Premium = 3% × ₹12,00,000 = ₹36,000
Amount payable = ₹12,36,000
