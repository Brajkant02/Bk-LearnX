Principles of Slide Design

  • One main idea per slide
  • Clear visual hierarchy
  • Readable typography
  • High contrast
  • Consistent colors and fonts
  • Balanced white space
  • Relevant visuals
  • Limited animation

Accessibility

  • Alt text
  • High contrast
  • Readable font size
  • Video captions
  • Logical reading order
  • Text labels with color

Common Mistakes

  • Too much text
  • Small font
  • Low contrast
  • Too many colors
  • Irrelevant images
  • Excessive animations
  • Inconsistent layouts
  • Reading slides word-for-word
