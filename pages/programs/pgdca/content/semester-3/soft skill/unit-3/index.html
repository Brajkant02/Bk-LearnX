1. Introduction to Writing Skill

Writing skill is the ability to express ideas, information, observations and messages clearly through written language. Good writing should be clear, organised, meaningful and appropriate to the purpose.

Picture Composition

Writing a meaningful description or short composition based on a picture.

Paragraph Writing

Developing one central idea in a connected group of sentences.

Notice Writing

Writing a short formal announcement to communicate important information.

Common Goal

Communicate information accurately, logically and effectively.

Exam Focus: Always pay attention to the given topic, word limit, format, sequence, grammar, spelling and relevance.

2. Picture Composition

Picture composition is a form of writing in which a student observes a picture carefully and develops a meaningful description, story or short composition based on what is visible in the picture.

Purpose

  • Tests observation skills.
  • Develops imagination and creativity.
  • Tests vocabulary and sentence formation.
  • Checks the ability to organise ideas.
  • Encourages students to connect visual information with written language.

What Can Be Included?

Element What to Observe
People Who is present? What are they doing?
Place Where is the scene taking place?
Objects Important things visible in the picture.
Action What is happening?
Time/Weather Morning, evening, rainy, sunny, etc., if visible.
Relationship How are people or objects connected?
Message What lesson or idea does the picture suggest?

3. How to Observe a Picture

  1. Look at the complete picture first. Understand the overall scene.
  2. Identify the main subject. Decide what the picture mainly shows.
  3. Observe the background. Note the place and surroundings.
  4. Identify people and objects. Mention only details that are relevant.
  5. Observe actions. Use suitable action verbs such as walking, playing, reading, helping, planting, working.
  6. Arrange ideas logically. Move from general description to specific details.
  7. Identify the possible message. If the picture has a social, environmental or moral theme, mention it.
  8. Write in complete sentences. Avoid disconnected words and random observations.
Important: Do not invent too many details that cannot reasonably be understood from the picture. Build your composition from visible clues.

4. Picture Composition – Suggested Format

Title: A Clean and Green Environment

Opening: The picture shows a clean public park surrounded by green trees.

Main Description: Several people are walking and children are playing in the park. Some people are sitting on benches while others are enjoying the pleasant surroundings. Dustbins are placed at suitable points, and the area appears clean and well maintained.

Message/Conclusion: The picture gives the message that clean and green public places make our surroundings healthier and more pleasant. Everyone should help keep public places clean.

Useful Sentence Starters

  • The picture shows...
  • The picture presents...
  • In the foreground...
  • In the background...
  • We can see...
  • There are...
  • Some people are...
  • The scene appears to be...
  • The picture conveys the message that...
  • Overall, the picture shows...

5. Picture Composition – Solved Examples

Example 1: Tree Plantation

Title: Plant Trees, Save the Future

The picture shows a group of students planting young trees in an open area. Some students are digging the soil while others are placing plants into the ground. A few students are watering the newly planted trees. The surroundings look clean and green. The activity shows the importance of protecting nature and increasing green cover. Tree plantation helps improve the environment and provides a better future for coming generations. The picture gives us the message that everyone should take responsibility for protecting trees and nature.

Example 2: Road Safety

Title: Road Safety Awareness

The picture presents a busy road where people are following traffic rules. A traffic signal is visible, and pedestrians are using a crossing. Vehicles are moving carefully and people appear to be respecting road signs. The scene highlights the importance of discipline and awareness while using roads. Following traffic rules can reduce accidents and protect lives. The picture gives the message that road safety is a shared responsibility.

Example 3: Cleanliness

Title: Keep Your Surroundings Clean

The picture shows people cleaning a public area. Some individuals are collecting waste while others are putting garbage into dustbins. The clean surroundings create a healthy and pleasant environment. The picture highlights that cleanliness cannot be maintained by one person alone. Citizens should avoid littering and should dispose of waste properly. The main message is that cleanliness is everyone's responsibility.

6. Paragraph Writing

A paragraph is a group of related sentences that develops one central idea. All sentences should be connected to the same topic.

Characteristics of a Good Paragraph

  • Unity: All sentences focus on one main idea.
  • Coherence: Ideas are connected logically.
  • Clarity: The meaning is easy to understand.
  • Conciseness: Avoid unnecessary repetition.
  • Correctness: Use proper grammar, spelling and punctuation.
  • Organisation: Present ideas in a logical sequence.

7. Structure of a Paragraph

1. Topic Sentence

Introduces the main idea of the paragraph.

2. Supporting Sentences

Explain, describe or provide examples related to the topic.

3. Concluding Sentence

Summarises the idea or gives a final thought.

Topic: Importance of Education

Topic Sentence: Education plays an important role in the development of an individual and society.

Supporting Sentences: It provides knowledge, develops skills and improves decision-making ability. Education helps people understand their rights and responsibilities. It also increases opportunities for employment and personal growth.

Concluding Sentence: Therefore, quality education is essential for building a knowledgeable, skilled and responsible society.

8. Rules and Techniques for Paragraph Writing

Rule Explanation
Understand the topic Know exactly what the paragraph is asking you to discuss.
Stay on topic Do not introduce unrelated ideas.
Use logical order Arrange ideas from introduction to explanation and conclusion.
Use linking words Use words such as firstly, moreover, however, therefore, finally.
Avoid repetition Express an idea once clearly rather than repeating it.
Check grammar Use correct tense, subject-verb agreement and sentence structure.
Check punctuation Use full stops, commas and other punctuation correctly.

Useful Linking Words

Adding: moreover, furthermore, also, in addition

Contrast: however, but, although, on the other hand

Cause: because, since, due to

Result: therefore, thus, hence, as a result

Sequence: firstly, secondly, next, finally

9. Solved Paragraphs

A. Importance of Time

Time is one of the most valuable resources in human life. Unlike money or material things, lost time cannot be recovered. Proper time management helps students complete their studies, responsibilities and personal activities in an organised manner. A timetable can help a student divide time between study, rest and recreation. People who respect time are usually better prepared to achieve their goals. Therefore, everyone should understand the value of time and make productive use of every day.

B. Importance of Computers

Computers have become an important part of modern life. They are used in education, business, banking, communication, healthcare and many other fields. Computers can process large amounts of information quickly and accurately. Students can use them to learn new skills, prepare assignments and access educational resources. However, computers should be used responsibly and safely. In conclusion, computers are powerful tools that can improve productivity and learning when used properly.

C. Environmental Protection

Environmental protection is essential for the survival and well-being of living beings. Air pollution, water pollution, deforestation and excessive waste can damage natural resources. People can help protect the environment by planting trees, saving water, reducing unnecessary plastic use and disposing of waste responsibly. Industries and governments also have an important role in controlling pollution and conserving resources. A clean environment provides healthier living conditions. Therefore, environmental protection should be treated as a shared responsibility.

D. Social Media

Social media has changed the way people communicate and share information. It allows users to connect with friends, communities and organisations quickly. Students can also use social platforms to discover educational content and exchange ideas. However, excessive or careless use may waste time and expose users to misinformation and privacy risks. People should therefore verify information, protect personal data and use social media responsibly. A balanced approach can help users gain benefits while reducing its disadvantages.

10. Notice Writing

A notice is a short formal written announcement used to communicate important information to a particular group of people.

Common Purposes of Notices

  • School or college events
  • Competitions
  • Meetings
  • Workshops and seminars
  • Lost and found information
  • Sports activities
  • Cultural programmes
  • Important instructions or announcements
  • Changes in schedule

11. Notice Writing Format

ABC COLLEGE

NOTICE

Date: 21 August 2026

INTER-COLLEGE QUIZ COMPETITION

All students are informed that an Inter-College Quiz Competition will be organised on 30 August 2026 at 11:00 a.m. in the college seminar hall. Students interested in participating should register their names with the undersigned by 27 August 2026.

Brajkant Sharma
Student Coordinator

Part Content
Name of Institution Name of school, college, organisation or authority.
NOTICE Clearly written heading.
Date Date on which the notice is issued.
Title Short and clear subject of the notice.
Body What, when, where, who, instructions and other necessary details.
Signature Name/signature of the issuing person.
Designation Position of the person issuing the notice.
5W + 1H: While writing a notice, try to cover What, When, Where, Who, Why and How whenever relevant.

12. Notice Writing Rules

Do's

  • Use a clear heading.
  • Write the date.
  • Use concise and formal language.
  • Include all essential information.
  • Keep the notice brief and informative.
  • Mention contact/registration details when necessary.
  • End with the name and designation of the issuing authority.

Don'ts

  • Do not write unnecessary background information.
  • Do not use informal chat language.
  • Do not leave out important dates, time or venue.
  • Do not make the notice excessively long.
  • Do not use confusing or ambiguous instructions.
Notice vs Paragraph: A paragraph develops an idea in detail, while a notice communicates specific information quickly and formally.

15. Quick Revision

Picture Composition: Observe → Identify → Organise → Describe → Conclude.

Paragraph: Topic Sentence → Supporting Ideas → Conclusion.

Notice: Institution → NOTICE → Date → Title → Body → Signature → Designation.

Picture Writing: Focus on visible details and the central message.

Paragraph Writing: Maintain unity, coherence, clarity and relevance.

Notice Writing: Be brief, formal, factual and complete.

Exam Mantra: Understand the question first. Follow the required format. Use simple and correct English. Keep ideas organised. Check spelling and punctuation before submitting.
