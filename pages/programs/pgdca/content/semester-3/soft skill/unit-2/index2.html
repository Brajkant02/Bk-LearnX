2.0 Types of Communication

The style, tone, word selection, length and delivery method of communication may depend on the situation, the receiver's status and the urgency of the message. Therefore, different communication methods are used for different situations.

Four Bases of Classification

  1. Style and purpose: Formal and Informal
  2. Language, media, method, expression: Verbal and Non-Verbal
  3. Method of delivery: Oral and Written
  4. Flow in organization hierarchy: Vertical (Upward/Downward), Horizontal and Diagonal/Crosswise

2.1 Verbal Communication

Meaning: Verbal communication means expressing feelings, knowledge, ideas and information by using words or language.

The development of language made communication more accurate, quick and clear. On the basis of the method of delivery, verbal communication is divided into two types:

Verbal Communication→Oral CommunicationWritten Communication

2.1.1 Oral Communication

Communication in which language is used and speech organs are used to deliver the message is called oral communication. Examples include gossip, speeches, telephonic conversations, seminars and interviews.

Advantages Disadvantages
  • Fast
  • Literacy/reading-writing required nahi
  • Feedback immediate
  • Writing skill required nahi
  • Record maintain karna difficult
  • Retake possible nahi
  • Emotional situations mein use difficult ho sakta hai
  • Good communication skill required

2.1.2 Written Communication

Communication in which language is used and writing capabilities are used to deliver the message is called written communication. According to the source, it has an important role in professional life.

Advantages Disadvantages
  • Permanent record ke liye useful
  • Rough draft ko finalise karne se pehle several times edit kiya ja sakta hai
  • Jahan oral communication possible nahi, wahan helpful
  • Message draft karne ke liye expertise required
  • Literacy required
  • Feedback immediate nahi hota

2.2 Non-Verbal Communication

Meaning: Words/language ke bina communication karna non-verbal communication hai. Isme face expressions, body language, gestures, symbols etc. use hote hain.

Non-verbal communication has been important since primitive times. When words are limited, facial expressions, postures, gestures, voice dynamics, space and symbols help convey meaning.

2.2.1 Body Language and Importance

Body language (kinesics) physical body movements ka study hai. Isme facial expressions, eye movements, gestures, posture and limb movements included hain.

  • It helps in understanding the real feelings of a person.
  • Communication ko complete karta hai.
  • It supports verbal communication.
  • Scope very wide hai.
  • It supports the message.
  • It projects a strong image of the presenter.

Eye Contact

  • It catches the attention of the audience.
  • It builds rapport with the audience.
  • It supports words and verbal communication.
  • It shows the speaker's confidence and the audience's interest.

Facial Expressions

  • Happiness, surprise, fear, anger and sadness jaise emotions express karte hain.
  • Natural and pleasant expression communication ko impressive banata hai.
  • Audience ko main point ki taraf attract karta hai.
  • Ye universal nature ke ho sakte hain.

Gestures

  • Communication effective banate hain.
  • Audience ka attention catch karte hain.
  • Verbal communication ko support karte hain.
  • Robotic image ko reduce karte hain.
  • Main points ko quickly understand karne mein help karte hain.
  • They strengthen the speaker's message.
  • They help keep the audience alert during a presentation.
  • They help avoid language barriers.

Posture

  • Speaker ke confidence ko reflect karta hai.
  • Correct posture spine par stress/strain reduce karta hai.
  • Confidence, submissiveness and openness jaise personality traits suggest kar sakta hai.
  • Proper alignment efficient working mein help karta hai.

2.2.2 Dressing Sense / Appearance

Dress selection personality, standard and taste ke baare mein message deti hai. Dressing occasion ke purpose ke according honi chahiye. Isme jewellery, make-up, shoes, hairstyle etc. bhi included ho sakte hain.

  • It communicates a person's standard.
  • It may bring applause or criticism.
  • It can make bonding with like-minded people easier.
  • Good dressing can create opportunities on the basis of impression.
  • It presents a visual image and communicates something about a person's character or personality.

2.2.3 Voice Dynamics

Speech verbal communication se related hai, while voice dynamics non-verbal communication ka part hai. Voice dynamics mein voice quality, rate, pitch, volume, speaking style, rhythm, intonation and stress included hain.

  • It reduces a monotonous tone.
  • It attracts the audience.
  • It adds mood and feeling to words.
  • It can give a sense of urgency to the voice.

2.2.4 Proxemics (Space Around You Language)

Proxemics is the study of spatial distance or physical space between the speaker and listener. It explains the use of physical space in interpersonal relations and in different cultures and situations.

Zone Meaning / Source Example
Intimate Zone The very close zone for people who are very close to you, such as a personal secretary, officers at horizontal levels and close friends.
Personal Zone People who are personally associated with you, such as friends, colleagues and relatives.
Social Zone People who may be known or unknown to you and who come in contact with you at social occasions or gatherings.
Public Zone People who listen to you in a public speech.

2.2.5 Time Language (Chronemics)

Chronemics time ke proper use ka study hai. The use of time creates an impression of a person's personality and professional behaviour.

  • Time management ka idea deta hai.
  • Punctuality aur appreciation ko promote karta hai.
  • It creates a good impression on others.
  • Time save karta hai.
  • Unnecessary conversation avoid karne mein help karta hai.
  • It helps keep communication precise and to the point.

2.2.6 Signs and Symbols

Signs and symbols are primitive but useful forms of non-verbal communication. They provide clues for particular statements and reduce the need for words.

  • They easily grab attention.
  • They can communicate more strongly than words or sentences.
  • They convey a message or information using less space.
  • They save time and provide quick expression.
  • They are easily noticeable to passersby.
  • Language barriers avoid karte hain.
  • Universal nature ho sakti hai.

2.3 Flow Based Communication

In the professional field, people at different posts communicate for coordination and smooth functioning within an organization.

HorizontalVertical → Upward / DownwardDiagonal / Crosswise

2.3.1 Horizontal Communication

When two people at the same status or hierarchy level discuss something to reach a conclusion or communicate with each other in an organization, it is called horizontal communication.

2.3.2 Vertical Communication

Communication between people at different hierarchy positions is called vertical communication. It has two types:

  • Vertically Downward: A person at a higher hierarchy level communicates with a person at a lower hierarchy level. Example: Engineer → Junior Engineer.
  • Vertically Upward: A person at a lower hierarchy level communicates with a person at a higher hierarchy level. Example: Junior Engineer → Engineer.

2.3.3 Diagonal / Cross Communication

When communication does not follow hierarchy-level rules and people at different levels communicate directly, it is called diagonal or cross communication. Example: The boss of a company directly calls an office clerk without informing the head of the clerical department.

2.4 Informal Communication

Informal communication is personal communication or communication without formality. It is used with relatives, friends, neighbours and well-known people in a personal style. It helps develop relationships and strong bonds and, according to the source, can help release stress and tension in family, society and the workplace.

2.5 Formal Communication

Formal communication is impersonal communication or communication with formality. It is used with people related to jobs or professions for professional purposes. It helps in the smooth functioning of an organization and fills the gap between departments. It is also called professional communication.

Basis Informal Formal
Audience Relatives, friends, very known people Professionals, work-related people, boss etc.
Language Local, friendly etc. Impersonal, specific, artificial
