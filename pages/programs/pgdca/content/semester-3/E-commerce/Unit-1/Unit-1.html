Introduction to E-Commerce

E-Commerce (Electronic Commerce) वह प्रक्रिया है जिसमें Internet के माध्यम से goods, services, information और online payments की buying, selling और exchange की जाती है।

Main Features

  • 24×7 Online Business
  • Global Market Reach
  • Fast Transactions
  • Online Payment Support
  • Lower Operating Cost

Supply Chain Management (SCM)

Supply Chain Management raw material procurement से लेकर product manufacturing, warehousing, transportation और customer delivery तक की पूरी process को efficiently manage करने की technique है.

Image Placeholder : Supply Chain Flow

Stages of SCM

  1. Supplier
  2. Manufacturer
  3. Warehouse
  4. Distributor
  5. Retailer
  6. Customer

Advantages of SCM

  • Lower inventory cost
  • Fast delivery
  • Better customer satisfaction
  • Improved product availability
  • Higher business efficiency
Exam Point: E-Commerce and Supply Chain Management work together to deliver products quickly, securely and efficiently to customers.
