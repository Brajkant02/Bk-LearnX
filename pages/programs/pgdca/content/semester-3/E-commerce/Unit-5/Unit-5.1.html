Google AdWords (Google Ads)

Introduction to Google Ads (AdWords)

Google Ads (formerly Google AdWords) is Google's online advertising platform. Businesses create campaigns to show ads on Google Search, YouTube and partner websites.

Content Structuring

Organize campaigns into logical categories. Each Ad Group should target one keyword theme.

Quality Score

Factor Description
Expected CTR Probability of clicks.
Ad Relevance Keyword and ad match.
Landing Page Useful, fast and relevant page.
Remember: Higher Quality Score often reduces Cost Per Click (CPC).
